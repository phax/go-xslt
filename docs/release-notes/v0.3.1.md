go-xslt 0.3.1 brings the desktop app to German, French and Italian, and makes the library faster and leaner: grouping with keys and XSD validation take about a third less time, everything else about a tenth.

## Desktop app

- **Languages**: the app speaks English, German, French and Italian. The settings choose one, or *System*, which follows your computer. Failed XSD assertions show their `msg:message` in that language when the schema provides one.

## Performance

The engine allocates less memory per run, which is where most of its time went. Results are unchanged; every W3C conformance number is the same as in 0.3.0.

| Workload (37 MB input, Apple M4) | 0.3.0 | 0.3.1 | libxslt / libxml2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| copy the document | 1.30 s | 1.16 s | 0.68 s |
| recursive apply-templates | 2.71 s | 2.42 s | 1.27 s |
| sort by two keys | 1.73 s | 1.53 s | 18.99 s |
| Muenchian grouping with `xsl:key` | 2.55 s | 1.41 s | 0.90 s |
| XPath predicates and functions | 1.21 s | 1.15 s | 0.85 s |
| XSD 1.0 validation | 2.24 s | 1.47 s | 0.51 s |

Peak memory is 50–300 MB lower per workload. Details on the [performance page](https://tim-riep.github.io/go-xslt/performance).

## Also

- Code coverage of the library is measured every night together with the W3C suites and published on [Codecov](https://codecov.io/gh/tim-riep/go-xslt).
